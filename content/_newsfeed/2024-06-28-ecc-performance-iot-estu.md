On **June 28, 2024**, the research article *"Performance Comparison of ECC Libraries for IoT Devices"* by **İsmet Kaan Çekiş**, **Arda Toros**, **Nilüfer Apaydın**, and **Dr. İlker Özçelik** was published in the **Eskişehir Technical University Journal of Science and Technology A - Applied Sciences and Engineering** (ESTÜ BİBİD-A).

This paper evaluates leading Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC) libraries (including Micro-ECC, TinyCrypt, and WolfSSL) across constrained microcontrollers such as STM32, measuring key exchange, digital signature generation, and verification latencies under limited memory constraints.
