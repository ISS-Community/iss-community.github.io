On **June 24, 2025**, the research article *"BEATS: Practical Audit Trail in Blockchain Systems"* by **Baraa Alagha** and **Dr. İlker Özçelik** was officially published in **IEEE Access**.

Modern enterprise workflows and distributed service meshes require immutable, verifiable audit logging without incurring prohibitive storage overhead or transaction verification bottlenecks on public/consortium blockchains. BEATS introduces a lightweight audit trail architecture combining cryptographic accumulators and constant-size membership proofs to compress high-frequency audit events into compact, tamper-evident cryptographic commitments.

#### Key Highlights

- **Constant-Size Proofs:** $O(1)$ membership witness generation independent of audit log volume.
- **Microsecond Verification:** Fast batch verification suitable for high-throughput enterprise pipelines.
- **Enterprise Blockchain Integration:** Practical deployment on Hyperledger Sawtooth and IPFS decentralized storage.
