On **December 19, 2025**, the research article *"Botnet Node Detection Using Graph Learning"* by **Gizem Karyağdı** and **Dr. İlker Özçelik** was officially published in the **Applied Sciences** journal (MDPI).

The paper addresses the detection of stealthy botnets by modeling structural network communication graphs using Graph Neural Networks (GNNs). By learning topological interaction patterns rather than relying solely on individual flow features, the model achieves high classification accuracy against evasive botnet infrastructure.

#### Publication Details

- **Authors:** Gizem Karyağdı, İlker Özçelik
- **Journal:** *Applied Sciences*, 2025, 16(1), 24 (Open Access)
- **DOI Link:** [10.3390/app16010024](https://doi.org/10.3390/app16010024)
