#### Seminar Overview

Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have emerged as powerful tools for identifying complex topological structures in cybersecurity, including botnet command-and-control (C2) infrastructures and multi-stage lateral movement. However, GNNs remain vulnerable to subtle graph structure perturbations and feature poisoning.

This seminar examines:
- Topological perturbation attacks and node injection strategies
- Gradient-based and heuristic adversarial attacks on graph classifiers
- Quantitative robustness metrics for mission-critical security environments
- Defensive mechanisms and robust graph learning architectures
