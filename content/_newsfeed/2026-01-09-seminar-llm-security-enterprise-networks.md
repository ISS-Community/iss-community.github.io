#### Presentation Summary

As enterprises integrate Large Language Models (LLMs) into internal workflows, communication backbones face novel threat vectors ranging from prompt injection and data exfiltration to unauthorized model extraction.

This webcast covers:
- Threat modeling for cloud and on-premise enterprise LLM deployments
- Preventing sensitive organizational telemetry leakage
- Architectural boundaries and inspection gateways for AI services
- Robust monitoring policies for enterprise ingress and egress points
