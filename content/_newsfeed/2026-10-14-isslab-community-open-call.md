Move beyond theory!

The Intelligent Systems Security Laboratory ([**#ISSLab**](https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/all/?keywords=%23isslab&origin=HASH_TAG_FROM_FEED)) is expanding. We are looking for highly motivated and ambitious **undergraduate** and **graduate** students to join our team.

If you are passionate about solving real-world security challenges, building on actual hardware, and kickstarting your academic career, this is your opportunity.

### What will you work on?

- **Hands-on Infrastructure:** Manage, program, and deploy solutions in our in-house data center using real hardware and containerized environments.
- **Industry-Sponsored R&D:** Contribute to massive-scale, real-world solutions in network defense and anomaly detection.
- **Defense-Industry AI Security:** Work on the cutting edge of AI defense, developing adversarial attack mitigation strategies.

### What's in it for you?

- Direct mentorship for TÜBİTAK student projects.
- Co-authoring papers for top-tier academic journals and conferences.
- An exclusive opportunity for an internship at a real operational data center in Istanbul for top-performing students.

Take the first step towards building a highly technical and competitive academic or industry career. The flyer details the research experience and opportunities available to dedicated student researchers.
