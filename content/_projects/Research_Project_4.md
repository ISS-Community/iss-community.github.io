<h4>Project Overview</h4>

Modern botnets and sophisticated cyber attacks frequently conceal their presence by disguising malicious traffic within benign communication streams. This ISS Lab research project develops **Graph Neural Network (GNN)** models that capture structural, behavioral, and topological relationships between network nodes.

By modeling host communication as dynamic graphs, our framework accurately detects compromised botnet nodes, malicious command-and-control (C2) channels, and coordinated multi-stage attacks even in the presence of traffic encryption and obfuscation.

**Lab Research:** Visit [esoguiss.wordpress.com](https://esoguiss.wordpress.com/)

#### Key Research Areas

- Graph representation learning for network flow topologies
- Host interaction modeling and dynamic graph construction
- Botnet node identification and C2 communication detection
- Robustness against adversarial traffic evasion techniques
- Real-time inference on streaming network topologies
