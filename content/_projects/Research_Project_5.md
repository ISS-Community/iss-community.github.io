<h4>Project Overview</h4>

Enterprise information systems require robust, tamper-proof logging mechanisms to ensure compliance, detect unauthorized modifications, and maintain verifiable forensics records. The **BEATS (Blockchain Enterprise Audit Trail System)** project develops a scalable architecture that leverages distributed ledgers to provide cryptographic proof of log immutability without the traditional storage and throughput bottlenecks of raw blockchain storage.

By combining optimized accumulator structures with decentralized consensus, BEATS enables high-throughput event logging and near-instant cryptographic verification for mission-critical operations.

**Lab Research:** Visit [esoguiss.wordpress.com](https://esoguiss.wordpress.com/)

#### Key Research Areas

- Scalable cryptographic audit trailing for distributed architectures
- Accumulator-based batch verification and compact proof generation
- Privacy-preserving log verification in multi-tenant environments
- Integration with edge computing and smart agriculture workflows
- Regulatory compliance and immutable digital forensics
